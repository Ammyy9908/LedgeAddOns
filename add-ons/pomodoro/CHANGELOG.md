# Changelog

Newest version first. Every new `version` in addon.json needs an entry here.

## 0.1.0

- A Pomodoro page in the notch: pick Focus or Break, set any length from a minute to eight hours with the slider or
  the − and + buttons, and press Start timer. Focus and Break each keep their own length.
- A stopwatch that counts up, from the stopwatch button.
- While it runs: pause, resume and stop on the page, with the time at the right.
- When a session reaches its length: the end sound and a "Pomodoro complete" notice, then it keeps counting overtime
  (+00:22) with a button to start the next session. Or turn on Move on automatically.
- The time beside the notch with a progress ring, on the lock screen, and on a Home widget that's also a tile in the
  Ledge iPhone app's Droplets, under Focus. Show seconds can be turned off to read in minutes.
- A keyboard shortcut to start, pause and resume, set under Shortcuts.
- An optional focus title for the session.
- An end sound picked from the macOS sounds, with a preview, or none.
- Brown, pink or white noise, switched on and off from the page, with a volume and a choice to play during breaks.
- Start instantly: opening the page starts your last timer.
