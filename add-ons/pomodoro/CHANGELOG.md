# Changelog

Newest version first. Every new `version` in addon.json needs an entry here.

## 0.1.0

- A Pomodoro page in the notch: pick Focus or Break, set any length from a minute to eight hours with the slider or
  the − and + buttons, and press Start timer. Focus and Break each keep their own length.
- While it runs: pause, resume and stop on the page, with the countdown at the right.
- The countdown beside the notch with a progress ring, on the lock screen, and on a Home widget that's also a tile in
  the Ledge iPhone app's Droplets, under Focus.
- A keyboard shortcut to start, pause and resume, set under Shortcuts.
- An optional focus title for the session.
- When a session ends: a chime, a notice, and a button to start the next one, or move on automatically.
- Brown or pink noise while the timer runs.
- Start instantly: opening the page starts your last timer.
