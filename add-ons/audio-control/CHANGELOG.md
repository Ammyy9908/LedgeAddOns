# Changelog

Newest version first. Every new `version` in addon.json needs an entry here.

## 0.1.0

- A page in the notch's Add-ons tab, and a widget for Home, with a row for each app that's playing sound: its icon,
  name and level, filled to the level. Drag or scroll on a row to change it.
- Turn an app up past 100%, to 200%: a line marks 100%, and the boost shows in orange.
- Remembers each app's level, so an app you turned down stays down the next time it plays.
- Settings: show paused apps, include system sounds, a Natural or Linear volume curve, allow boost above 100%,
  and reset all volumes.
- Output per app: send an app to its own speakers or headphones; its row shows where it plays.
- Preferred devices: up to three outputs the Mac switches to, best first, when one of them connects.
- Microphones: mute every microphone from Settings, the button on the page or a keyboard shortcut you pick (⌃⌥M,
  ⌥⌘M or ⌃⌥⌘M); a notice beside the notch says which. Turning the add-on off unmutes them.
